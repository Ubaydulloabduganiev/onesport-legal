OneSport Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Status: draft. This reflects what the OneSport app actually does today. It will be revised the moment real accounts, bookings, or payments go live — see “What happens next” below.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the OneSport mobile app (package uz.onesport.app), developed by Ubaydullo Abduganiev (“we”, “us”). It covers what happens when you install and use the app.

What data we collect today

None, beyond two on-device settings. The app currently stores only your chosen theme (light/dark) and language (Uzbek/Russian/English) using Android's local on-device storage. This data:

  • Stays on your device — it is never transmitted to us or anyone else.
  • Is not linked to you, your name, or any account (the app has no accounts yet).
  • Is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. We do not show ads.

Permissions

The app requests one permission: Internet access. This is reserved for future app updates and content delivery — the current version does not send or receive any personal data over the network.

Sign-up / phone number / OTP screens

The app currently shows phone-number and verification-code screens for demonstration purposes only. No phone number or code you enter is transmitted anywhere or stored anywhere — these screens are not yet connected to a real account system.

Children's privacy

OneSport is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age, since it collects no personal information at all in its current form.

Data retention & deletion

Since we don't collect anything beyond your local device settings, there is nothing for us to retain or for you to request deletion of. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

What happens next

Once real accounts, bookings, or payments are added, this policy must be rewritten to accurately describe:

  • What personal data is collected (e.g. name, phone number, booking history)
  • Who it's shared with (e.g. gyms you book with, a payment processor)
  • How long it's retained and how you can request its deletion
  • Any analytics or crash-reporting tools added

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too. We recommend checking back periodically.
