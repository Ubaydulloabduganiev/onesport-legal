OneSport Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Status: draft. This reflects what the OneSport app actually does today, now that real accounts (phone sign-in), cloud-synced favorites/bookings/plan, onboarding profile data (name, goal, target and timeframe), referral codes, an on-device profile photo, and in-app account deletion are live. See “What happens next” below for what's still not covered.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the OneSport mobile app (package uz.onesport.app), developed by Ubaydullo Abduganiev (“we”, “us”). It covers what happens when you install and use the app.

What data we collect

Account data (once you sign in)

To use OneSport, you sign in with your phone number. We use Firebase Authentication (a Google service) to send you a one-time SMS verification code and confirm it's really your number. We store:

  • Your phone number, linked to your account.
  • The display name you provide during onboarding, or later edit from your profile.
  • The goal you choose when you first sign in (e.g. lose weight, build muscle, grow taller, or a goal you write in your own words), its target if it has one (e.g. 15 kg or 5 cm), and the timeframe you give yourself (e.g. 1 month). We don't verify any of these, and you don't have to share anything you'd rather not — a goal can be as general as “stay fit”.
  • The gyms and classes you mark as favorites.
  • Your booking history (which class/gym visit, and when).
  • Your purchased pass plan, if any (currently cosmetic — see “Payments” below; no real payment is processed yet), and, if you freeze it, the date it's frozen until.
  • A referral code we generate for your account, whether you've redeemed someone else's referral code (each account can redeem one), and any bonus days credited from that.

This data is stored in Cloud Firestore (also a Google/Firebase service), in a database located in the Mumbai (India) region, and is tied to your account so it's available across devices when you sign in with the same phone number. It is protected by database security rules so that only your own signed-in account can read or write it — not other users, and not the public — except where described in “Data visible to other users” below.

We do not collect your email, contacts, or payment details. Your profile photo, if you add one, stays on your device — see “On-device only” below.

Data visible to other users

One feature is inherently something you do with another OneSport user, so a limited amount of data crosses accounts:

  • Referral codes. Your referral code, on its own, is designed to be shared by you (e.g. via the app's own share sheet) and entered by someone else — that's how it's meant to work. It is stored in a way any signed-in account can look up, so a code can be matched back to your account when someone redeems it.

We do not otherwise make your profile, goal, favorites, bookings, or booking history visible to other users.

Membership transfers to another person are handled by our operator by phone, not inside the app. When you call, the operator sees your phone number and the recipient's number you give them, and uses them only to carry out that transfer. (An earlier version of the app had an in-app transfer and a membership marketplace that showed your phone number to the other party or to marketplace visitors; both have been removed.)

On-device only, never sent anywhere

  • Theme (light/dark), language (Uzbek/Russian/English), and your notification and sound settings — stored locally via Android's DataStore. Not linked to your account, not transmitted anywhere, deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.
  • Profile photo, if you add one — you pick it with Android's own photo picker, which gives the app access to that one photo only. A resized copy is saved in the app's private storage on your phone and is never uploaded, so it won't appear if you sign in on another device. You can remove it at any time from your profile; it's also deleted when you delete your account or uninstall the app.
  • Approximate city, if you grant location permission — used only to show which city you're in on the Home screen (e.g. “Tashkent”). This is resolved entirely on your device (coarse location + on-device reverse geocoding) and is never sent to us or stored anywhere — nothing leaves your phone. You can decline this permission; the app falls back to a default city label.

What we don't do

We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services, and we do not show ads. (If that changes — e.g. we add Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics later — this policy will be updated first.)

Permissions

  • Internet access — required for account sign-in and syncing your favorites/bookings/plan to Firestore.
  • Coarse location (optional) — used only for the on-device city label described above. You can deny it; the app still works.

Adding a profile photo doesn't need a storage permission: Android's photo picker only shares the single photo you choose.

Third parties / data processors

We use Firebase (Google) to run the parts of OneSport described above: Firebase Authentication (phone verification) and Cloud Firestore (storing your account data). Google processes this data on our behalf, governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's terms. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Payments

OneSport does not currently process real payments. The pass/plan purchase flow in the app is cosmetic — selecting a plan or a payment method (bank card, Uzum, Click, Payme, Paynet) does not charge you, move any money, or send anything to those providers, and the payment method you pick isn't stored. This will be updated here, in detail, before any real payment processing goes live.

Data retention & deletion

Your account data (phone number, display name, goal, favorites, bookings, plan, referral/promo data) is retained for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account yourself in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and your profile, goal, favorites, bookings, plan, referral code, any membership listings from the earlier marketplace, and your on-device profile photo. For security, you may be asked to sign in again first if you haven't recently.

If you can't use the app, you can also request deletion by contacting us at domicabduganiev@gmail.com from the phone number associated with your account (or otherwise confirming you're the account holder), and we will delete it. We aim to complete deletion requests within a reasonable time and will let you know once it's done. Records of transfers made with the earlier in-app transfer feature involve another person's account too and aren't removed automatically; contact us if you'd like them deleted.

Uninstalling the app removes your local settings and profile photo but does not delete your account data from Firestore — use “Delete account” for that (account data is kept otherwise so you can sign back in on a new device and pick up where you left off).

Children's privacy

OneSport is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child's account data has been collected, we will delete it — contact us at the email below to report this.
